# Ternary Check v0.2.3: source and interpretation review

Reviewed September 26, 2026 UTC. This is a targeted scientific and editorial review of the 14-entry retrieved corpus, with full software coverage of its 88 records; it is not a new systematic review of every primary claim or a certification of the complete publication archive.

## Changes and stronger context

- **RTA provenance:** The article record now links the Cell publication, retaining the open manuscript for the detailed reporter/DC50 comparisons. The essay distinguishes the approximately 81-fold reporter EC50 range from the nonsignificant DC50 comparison, which does not establish equivalence ([Cell](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(26)00936-0); [open manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13228303/)).
- **Interface convention:** The essay distinguishes half of protein-pair ΔSASA from summed burial, and from ligand-inclusive extended burial. Similar numeric values are not presented as biological validation ([Gadd et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5392356/)).
- **Ubiquitination geometry:** The complete-machine discussion uses Crowe et al. as additional context, with the 18.4 Å separation explicitly not described as a bond-forming configuration. K456R redundancy is distinguished from the effect of the K368/K445/K456 region perturbation ([Crowe et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11468923/)).
- **Catalytic clocks:** The approximately 100 s⁻¹ result is restricted to primed HIF1α peptide chain-elongation kinetics, not initial ubiquitination or cellular depletion ([Liwocha et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41594-023-01206-1)).
- **RNF39:** The primary paper's screen contains 960 analogs, not 60. Z5000181945 domain degradation is not attributed to Z4999952733 or extrapolated to full-length RNF39 ([Galli, Xiao et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-026-03237-7)).
- **SuFEx:** The measured ENL-dependent competition shift and stereoisomer comparison retain assay scope. dHTC2 modality remains unresolved and dHTC3 remains glue-like rather than structurally settled ([Shaum et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41589-025-02137-2)).
- **Database meaning:** Known versus predicted interaction evidence remains distinct, and neither becomes a universal induced-degradation ranking ([UbiBrowser 2.0](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/50/D1/D719/6406468)).

## Model-derived statements

The capacity inequality, the sqrt(Kt × Ke) equilibrium optimum, and the analytic post-washout recovery are derived from this application's stated equations. The synthetic capacity example uses 100 nM target, 8-hour half-life, 3 nM E3 and kpr 1.2 h⁻¹: synthesis 8.66434 nM/h, maximal processing 3.6 nM/h, and a necessary steady-state target-loss bound of 41.5496%. This is not a fitted compound claim.

The independent two-hazard model is monotonic in residence time and cannot establish an intermediate optimum. The essay therefore qualifies rather than reproduces the broader residence-band language in the prior landscape article. The PROTAC model does not quantitatively represent the covalent-glue, LOCKTAC, RNA-suppression or lysosomal-routing cases.

The earlier GlueMap/article ranking and substrate conflict remains quarantined. MG6 remains article-only. Detailed KAT2A compound-number claims retain their article-reported status. The heme–BACH1 study and eLife dynamics work retain their preprint/reviewed-preprint labels. Two archive essays still lack confirmed public article URLs. No current clinical-program status or imported real-compound parameter is inferred.

The case concordance is mechanically generated from the library and checked against its record IDs. That verifies coverage and traceability, not the truth of every underlying article statement.
